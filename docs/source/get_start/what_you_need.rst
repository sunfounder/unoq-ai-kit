What You Need
=============

Everything the course needs arrives in one box. Three boards do all the work, and each one has a page of its own — open it whenever you want the pin tables and the full specifications.

**Arduino UNO Q** — the brain

.. image:: /get_start/img/uno_q_front.png
    :width: 50%
    :align: center

Two processors in one board: a Linux side that runs Python, App Lab and the AI models, and a microcontroller side that switches pins in real time. See :ref:`cpn_uno_q` for the pin map.

**Robot Shield** — the muscle

.. image:: /get_start/img/robot_shield.png
    :width: 50%
    :align: center

Stacks on top of the UNO Q and adds the motor and servo drivers, the power circuit and the labelled 3-pin headers for sensors. See :ref:`cpn_robot_shield`.

**AVIO Carrier** — the senses

.. image:: /get_start/img/avio_carrier.png
    :width: 50%
    :align: center

Plugs into the UNO Q's high-speed connectors and brings the camera, the microphone, the speaker and the RGB LEDs. See :ref:`cpn_avio_carrier`.

**In the box**

* the three boards, already stacked in the photos above
* two servos on the pan-tilt mount, with the camera
* a USB-C cable
* the structural parts and screws

**You also need**

* a computer with a free USB port and a modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox
* an internet connection — App Lab and the course packages are downloaded from the network

Every part the lessons use has a page of its own: see :ref:`components`.
